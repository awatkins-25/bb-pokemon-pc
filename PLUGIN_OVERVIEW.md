Build your own party of Pokémon agents and watch them earn activity EXP as you work in BB.

## What you get

- An empty party with a + builder: choose any of 493 Pokémon from Gen I–IV.
- Editable agent names, roles, missions, and instructions; six agents or more, up to 100 saved profiles.
- Optional Porygon Build, Porygon2 Operate, and Porygon-Z Repair modes.
- Persistent tasks with eight ball skins, completion history, and reversible clearing.
- Lead sprites on chat rows and an animated EXP display above each chat. Collapse the scene into a thin line or pause its animations.

## How it works

Create profiles in the party, then choose a chat’s lead and supporting perspectives. Profiles apply at the next provider-session construction. Successful tool work earns XP; completed plan steps trigger levels. Providers without structured plans level up on successful turn completion. Activity XP is not a percentage estimate of task completion. Tasks also work through `bb pokemon-pc`.

## Requirements and limits

Requires BB 0.49+, SDK 0.6.132+, and the separate [Apple Notes plugin](https://github.com/alanagoyal/apple-notes-bb-theme). Uncached artwork needs internet access to [PokeAPI sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). No external account or paid service is required for the theme itself. Profiles do not install tools, grant account access, isolate private data, or launch separate workers. This project is unaffiliated with Pokémon’s rights holders.
